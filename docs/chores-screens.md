# Chores rota — the screens

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-01. Built for iOS and the web together. Works like
Bills (docs/bills-screens.md) so the two feel the same.

## Where it lives

In each group. The group's card shows your next turn ("Your turn: Bathroom"); the
group's page has a **Chores** section with this period's turns and a tick circle each.

## A chore

A name (Bathroom, Kitchen, Trash, Vacuuming), how often (weekly by default; every
2 weeks, monthly), who takes part (everyone in the group by default), and its size:
**Small 1 point · Medium 2 · Big 3**.

## Turns

- **Rotates every period**: each chore moves to the next person in the list.
- On the day, the person whose turn it is gets a notification ("Your turn: Bathroom").
  They tick it when done; everyone sees "Done ✓ by Nina".
- **Swap**: ask someone to take your turn; it changes only when they accept. Whoever
  does it gets the points.
- **Skip** (away, sick): the turn passes on, and your next turn comes back to you, so it stays fair.
- **Not ticked by the end of the period**: marked "missed by Ali" in the history, no
  points, and the rota moves on as planned.

## Points

Each chore done earns its points. A **monthly leaderboard** per group shows points and
chores done per person, with a history of who did what and when. No prizes; it resets each month.
