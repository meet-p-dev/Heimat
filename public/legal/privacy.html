Privacy Policy — Heimat

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Heimat is a free, non-commercial app for international students, made by one person: Meet Patel (patelmeet.2905@gmail.com), who is the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR. This policy covers the Heimat iOS app, Android app and website.

Data that never leaves your device

Your name, home country and currency, host currency, your funds-runway figures and every work shift you log are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded, not backed up by us, and disappear when you uninstall the app.

Data shared with your flat

When you create or join a flat, the following is stored on our server so your flatmates' devices can see it:

  • the flat's name and join code;
  • the display name you chose for yourself in that flat;
  • shared expenses — description, amount, currency, category, date, who paid, who it is split among;
  • recorded settlements between flatmates;
  • shared shopping/to-do list items and any custom categories.

This is visible to the other members of that flat, and to nobody else. Hosting is Supabase (PostgreSQL), in the EU (Ireland).

Your account

On first launch Heimat signs you in anonymously — no email, no password, no sign-up screen. If you later choose "Save account", we additionally store the email address you give, so you can sign back into the same flat on another device. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. We never see your password.

Notifications

If you turn notifications on, we store a push token for that device so the server can tell you when a flatmate adds a shared expense or records a payment to you. Delivery runs through Apple's Push Notification service on iOS, Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android, or your browser's push service on the web. Turning notifications off in the app deletes the token; you can also switch them off in your device settings at any time.

Exchange rates

To show amounts in your home currency, the app asks open.er-api.com for a reference rate. That request contains nothing but the two currency codes.

What Heimat does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no third-party trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No bank connection. Heimat never holds, moves or has access to your money.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Keeping and deleting data

Flat data is kept for as long as the flat exists. Leaving a flat in the app removes your membership from it. To have your account and everything attached to it deleted, email patelmeet.2905@gmail.com and it will be done within 30 days. Under the GDPR you can also ask for a copy of your data, or for it to be corrected.

Children

Heimat is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
