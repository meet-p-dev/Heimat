What Heimat is

A tracker. It helps you split flat costs with your flatmates, see how long your funds will last, and keep an eye on your student work hours. It never moves money. Balances, "who owes whom" figures and settle-up suggestions are a record of what you and your flatmates typed in — settling up happens between you, outside the app.

The work-hour figures are a guide, not legal advice

The work limits Heimat tracks are a convenience feature based on the usual rules for international students in Germany. Rules change, and they differ by visa, employer and university. Heimat's green/amber/red verdict is not legal advice and is not a substitute for checking with your employer, your university or the Ausländerbehörde. Heimat is not affiliated with any government body.
