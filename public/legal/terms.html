What Splitlife is

A record-keeping app. It helps you split costs with flatmates, groups and friends, keep track of shared bills and chores, and keep an eye on your student job. It never moves money. Balances, “who owes whom” and settle-up suggestions are a record of what you and the people you share with typed in — paying each other back happens between you, outside the app.

Guides, not advice

  • Work limits. The 140-day year (or 280 half days) and the 20-hour week follow the rules for international students in Germany. Rules change and differ by permit, university and employer. Splitlife’s verdict is not legal advice; check with your employer, your university or the Ausländerbehörde.
  • Tax estimate. The pay and tax figures are an estimate from what you enter, not tax advice. Your payslip and your tax office are what count.
  • Scanned receipts. Text recognition can misread a figure. Check the amounts before you save an expense.

Splitlife is not affiliated with any government body, university or bank.
