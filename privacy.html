Privacy Policy — MoneyTrack (personal bank-sync)

Last updated: June 2026

MoneyTrack is a personal-use, self-hosted finance tool operated by an individual for private use by the operator and a small number of personal contacts. It is not a commercial service.

What data is processed

With your explicit consent, the tool reads account and transaction information from your own bank via Enable Banking under PSD2 (account-information service, read-only — it cannot move money). It does not collect data about anyone who has not connected their own account.

Where it is stored

Transaction data is stored locally on the operator's own device and in the user's own browser storage. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. No advertising or tracking.

Consent & control

Bank access is granted by you at your bank's own login screen and expires automatically (PSD2 re-consent ~every 90 days). You can withdraw access at any time via your bank or Enable Banking.
